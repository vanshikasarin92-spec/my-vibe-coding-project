My own Design & Engineering teams don't use the accessibility hub because it's organized by source (WCAG, EN 301 549, ACA, guides), not by job. People can't tell what applies to their role and their current task, so accessibility gets checked at the end of a release instead of built in.

## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup. Their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

- **What they thought it did:** Their feedback was about how it looked, not about confusion over its purpose, which suggests they read it as an accessibility reference hub.
- **What surprised them:** The ‘jump to’ section seems to include all the same information as the left-hand sidebar, but it uses slightly different language to describe each step/section. 
- **The gap between what I intended and what they read:** As a user, they were not sure if they should be clicking into the links in this jump-to section or just following the natural hierarchy of the left-hand sidebar.
